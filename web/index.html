🛡️ Privacy Policy & Profile Isolation

1. Purpose-Limited Career Personalization

All data collected by CyberMentor (conversations, resume reviews, skill assessments, interview performance, and study plans) is used solely and exclusively to optimize your user experience and adapt ongoing career coaching within the application. We never sell, broker, or share your data.

2. Zero-Knowledge & Complete Profile Isolation

Every user profile is partitioned and isolated. Cloud Firestore security rules enforce that only your authenticated account can access, query, or modify your records. Administrator staff and third parties have zero access to your private conversation transcripts or individualized ACE memory notes.

3. Right to Erasure (One-Click Data Deletion)

You own your data. You can permanently purge all stored conversations, ACE memory notes, documented competencies, and career milestones at any time by clicking "Delete All My Data" in the sidebar.

4. Anonymized & Decoupled ACE Learning

CyberMentor continuously improves using Differential Privacy. Pedagogical strategies are stripped of all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) before being aggregated into a decoupled global coaching heuristic pool. Deleting your profile permanently erases your personal data while leaving zero traceable artifacts.
