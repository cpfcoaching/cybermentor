Account & Data Deletion

CyberMentor — by Breaking Into Cybersecurity / Christophe Foulon

At CyberMentor, we are committed to complete user data privacy, zero-knowledge profile isolation, and your absolute right to erasure under GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play Data Safety policies.

1. How to Delete Your Account & Associated Data

Option A: Instant In-App One-Click Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the CyberMentor application on your mobile device or web browser.
  2. Log in to your account if prompted.
  3. Open the Candidate Profile / Settings menu in the top right.
  4. Click the red "🗑️ Delete All My Data" button and confirm.

Result: Your account, chat history, resume data, and ACE learning records are permanently erased from production databases in real time.

Option B: Request Deletion via Email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit an email deletion request directly to our engineering team:

  • Email: support@breakingintocybersecurity.org
  • Subject Line: CyberMentor Account Deletion Request
  • Information Required: The email address associated with your Google SSO or CyberMentor account.

Requests sent via email are verified and processed within 24-48 business hours.

2. What Data Is Permanently Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following categories of data are completely and irreversibly purged:

  • User Profile & Authentication Data: User IDs, email addresses, and account credentials.
  • Conversational History: All chat logs, coaching transcripts, and voice interactions.
  • Career Artifacts & Resumes: Uploaded resume files, parsed skill matrices, and NIST NICE work role maps.
  • ACE Cognitive Memory Notes: Customized study schedules, certification milestones, and personalized mentorship notes.
  • Mock Interview Results: Rubric evaluation scores, recordings, and performance diagnostics.

3. Data Retention Period & Inactivity Purge

  • Immediate Hard Deletion: On-demand deletion requests are executed immediately across Cloud Firestore and active server caches.
  • Automated 90-Day Purge: Any inactive account that has not been logged into for 90 consecutive days is automatically and permanently purged.
  • Retained Data: No personally identifiable information (PII) is kept. Only fully anonymized, aggregated statistical metrics (e.g. total certification roadmaps generated across all users) may be retained for system performance analysis.
