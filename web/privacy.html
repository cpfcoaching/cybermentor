Part 1 — Privacy Policy & Data Protection Charter

1. Scope & Hosting Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the services, websites, and applications operated under breakingintocybersecurity.org and all subdomains, specifically including the CyberMentor AI coaching platform hosted at agent.breakingintocybersecurity.org (collectively, the "Platform").

The Platform is served and secured globally using Cloudflare reverse proxy and edge security services (providing TLS 1.3 encryption, DDoS mitigation, and Web Application Firewall protection) backed by containerized instances hosted on Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Cloud Firestore).

2. Data Collection & Purpose Specification

We believe in strict data minimization. We collect only what is necessary to authenticate users and generate personalized cybersecurity mentorship:

  • Authentication & Profile Data: When signing in with Google SSO (Firebase Authentication), we receive non-sensitive identity metadata (your name, email address, avatar photo, and unique Firebase user ID). We do not request or access third-party write scopes or sensitive permissions for students.
  • Career & Learning Preferences: Work role targets aligned with the NIST NICE Framework, target certifications, skill self-assessments, and study streak milestones.
  • Career Artifacts & Coaching Prompts: Uploaded resume excerpts and interactive coaching queries submitted to CyberMentor.

3. Zero Foundation Model Training & Ephemeral AI Processing

Your Privacy with Generative AI: Prompts, interview simulation answers, and resume texts submitted on agent.breakingintocybersecurity.org are transmitted ephemerally to Google Gemini AI models via Google Cloud solely to generate real-time feedback. Your personal prompts and candidate data are never sold, never shared with third-party advertisers, and never used to train or fine-tune public foundation AI models.

4. Zero-Knowledge & Profile Isolation

Every candidate's progress and conversation history are isolated in Cloud Firestore using authenticated user ID security rules (request.auth.uid == userId). No other user or unauthorized administrative personnel can access your private career roadmaps or interview scores.

5. Unconditional Right to Erasure & Retention

  • 1-Click Instant Deletion: You can permanently wipe your entire history, ACE cognitive reflections, and skill profile at any time in-app via the "Delete All My Data" button or at agent.breakingintocybersecurity.org/delete-account.html.
  • Automated 90-Day Inactivity Purge: Unused accounts inactive for 90 consecutive days are automatically and irreversibly purged from our database.

6. Security & Encryption Standards

All traffic is encrypted in transit using modern TLS 1.3 through Cloudflare edge SSL/TLS. Stored database records are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
